# Neutrality Principles

Butterfly City presents policy trade-offs and consequences. It does not take political sides. These rules apply to all UI text, event cards, citizen templates, ending text, README, code comments, and documentation.

## Do

- Present trade-offs and consequences: who benefits, who bears the cost, and when.
- Describe events factually: date, what happened, measured impact, and source.
- Present contested parameters as a range across different studies, with each source cited.
- Show the same level of detail for benefits and costs of every policy tool.
- Use international cases (Vienna, Singapore, Hong Kong) as reference points with their own trade-offs, not as models to copy or warnings to avoid.

## Do not

- Evaluate political parties, politicians, or any administration.
- Name parties or politicians in event cards, citizen text, or the ending, unless a name is part of an official policy title.
- Write who was right or wrong, or attribute blame or credit for an outcome.
- Use loaded or judgmental wording (e.g., "failed", "disastrous", "brilliant", "finally"). Use neutral, measurable language.
- Score the player's choices as morally good or bad. The ending shows outcomes and distribution, not a verdict.

## Required disclosure

Shown clearly in the UI:

> Initial data and events come from real open data. Policy outcomes are simulated, not predicted.

## Review check

Before finalizing any user-facing text, check: Would a reader from any political position find this a fair description of what happened and what the trade-offs are? If not, rewrite it.
