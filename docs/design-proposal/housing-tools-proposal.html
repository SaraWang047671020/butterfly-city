預算條與兩個新政策：設計稿

Budget bar and two new tools: design proposal

預算變成真正的限制以後，玩家要隨時看得到「今年還剩多少錢」，以及一次調整會花掉多少。這份設計稿有四個畫面：預算條（電腦版、手機版）、政策視窗裡的花費說明、包租代管視窗、囤房稅視窗。

Now that the budget is the real limit, players need to see how much of this year's money is left, and what one change costs. Four screens: the Budget_Bar (wide and narrow), the cost lines of the Policy_Dialog, the sublease dialog and the holding-tax dialog.

畫面上的 2015 年金額是遊戲裡真實歷史的數字；包租代管和囤房稅視窗裡的效果數字是示意，會在做完模擬規則後換成真的計算結果。 The 2015 amounts are the game's real-track figures. The effect figures in the sublease and holding-tax dialogs are illustrative and will be replaced by the computed results once the rules exist.

1. 預算條（電腦版，1280）1. Budget_Bar, wide (1280)

放在季度列的第二行，跟「下一季」在同一個區塊，玩家每一季都會看到。左邊是剩下的錢（最重要的數字），右邊是全年總額。每段寫出政策名稱和金額，不只靠顏色分辨；斜線紋路代表「已承諾、之後幾季才會付」的錢（例如社宅分好幾年付款），灰色斜線是還沒用的錢。

The second row of the turn bar, in the same block as Next quarter, so it is in view every quarter. Left: the money left (the key number); right: the year's total. Each segment is named in the legend with its amount, not by colour only; hatching marks money committed for later quarters (for example social housing paid over several years); grey hatching is money not used.

3. 政策視窗：按「套用」之前就看到花費3. Policy_Dialog: the cost before Apply

輸入數字後，欄位下面馬上出現一行藍框：這次要花多少、今年還剩多少。錢不夠時改成紅框，寫出還缺多少，並列出其他政策今年各花了多少，提示可以從哪裡挪。預算條上的這一段也會先用虛線預覽。

As soon as a value is typed, a blue line under the field gives the cost of the change and what stays this year. When it does not fit, the line turns red, gives the amount missing and lists what the other tools spend this year, so the player sees where money could come from. The bar previews the change with a dashed segment.

4. 包租代管視窗4. Sublease dialog

跟租金補貼視窗一樣，一個城市一個欄位。上方的「誰受益／誰負擔」兩邊寫得一樣詳細。欄位下的效果說明寫出平均每戶省多少房租，以及資料的限制（媒合到入住的時間查不到實際資料）。

Like the rent subsidy dialog, one field per city. Who benefits and who bears the cost are written with the same detail. Under the field, the effect line gives the average rent saved per household and states the data limit (no measured time from matching to move-in).

5. 囤房稅視窗5. Holding-tax dialog

囤房稅不花預算，而是改變稅收：多收的錢加進下一年的預算，少收則下一年變少。所以藍框改寫「下一年的預算會多／少多少」。稅率只能在當年的法定範圍內調整，拉桿兩端寫出法條。

The holding tax spends no budget; it changes revenue, and revenue above or below real history moves next year's budget. The blue line therefore gives the change to next year's budget. Rates stay within the statutory range of the date, named at the slider ends.

  • 市政府預算：多收的稅加進下一年的預算。City budget: extra revenue is added to next year's budget.
誰負擔WHO BEARS THE COST
  • 持有多戶或空屋的屋主：每年房屋稅變多。Owners of several or vacant homes: higher house tax each year.
  • 房東可能把部分稅轉嫁到房租上（研究結果不一，用範圍表示）。Landlords may pass part of the tax on to rent (the research disagrees, so it is shown as a range).
