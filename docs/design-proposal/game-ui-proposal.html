Butterfly City: game UI Design_Proposal

Status: PROPOSAL, NOT APPROVED. Game-UI spec task 12.1. No file in src/ui/ or src/styles/ exists. All text in the mocks is placeholder text. Map shapes are the real districts.json geometry. Map values are illustrative: computed from real history data for 2025Q4 outside the engine, not engine output. Fonts are system stacks; no external font or asset is used.

Disclosure shown in the header of every mock: Initial data and events come from real open data. Policy outcomes are simulated, not predicted.

0. Choices you must approve

  1. Theme and palette (section 2): dark "command centre" palette, accent cyan, yellow focus ring, red used only for risk and rejection.
  2. Typography (section 3): system font stacks only, 7 sizes, 3 weights.
  3. Layout per width (section 1): at 360 and 768 the Turn_Bar is fixed to the bottom of the viewport; at 1280 and 1920 it sits under the header. The Disclosure_Text is the second line of the header at every width.
  4. Map styling (section 4): casing + ring outline states, hatched "no value" pattern, diamond estimate marker, label omission rule, class number shown by pattern density.
  5. Two colour ramps (section 5): one sequential ramp (rent burden, price to income, commute time, low-use housing rate) and one diverging purple-to-teal ramp (population change), both with 7 classes and a pattern per class.
  6. Indicator_Class bounds (section 5): 5 sets of bounds, all PROPOSALS. The commute time bounds are the weakest and need a re-check on engine output.
  7. Ghost visuals (section 8): ghost glyph, display cap of 12 glyphs per district (proposal), "+N" overflow label, exact total always shown as text.
  8. Risk escalation (section 8): 4 levels, count thresholds 1 / 5 / 15 / 30 ghosts per district (proposal), localized, no full-screen effect.
  9. Reduced-motion equivalents (section 9): every transition becomes 0 ms with the same information shown statically.
  10. Focus and keyboard states (section 10), including the map ring (dark casing + yellow dashed).
  11. UI text decisions of task 12.2 (not part of this artifact): the 500-entry Timeline cap message and the limit "reason" display.

1. Game_Screen layout at 360, 768, 1280 and 1920 CSS pixels

Each mock is shown at its true width inside a scrolling frame. The dashed orange line marks the end of the initial viewport (360 × 740, 768 × 1024, 1280 × 800, 1920 × 1080). Regions: header with Disclosure_Text, Turn_Bar, Map_View with legend, District_Panel, Policy_Panel, Timeline_View, District_List.

  • 360: one column. Header (Disclosure_Text wraps to 3 lines, inside the viewport). Turn_Bar fixed at the bottom of the viewport with two 44 px buttons, so Next quarter, Fast-forward, Planning quarter and Displayed quarter stay visible at every scroll position (16.4). The legend is a details control (one activation, 16.2).
  • 768: one column for the map, then District_Panel and Policy_Panel side by side. Turn_Bar fixed at the bottom.
  • 1280: two columns: map and timeline left; panels and list right. Turn_Bar sticky under the header (top).
  • 1920: three columns: map and timeline; District_Panel and list; Policy_Panel.

8. Ghost_Layer, ghost visuals and risk escalation

The Ghost_Layer is an aria-hidden SVG group above the district shapes, without pointer events. In the game-ui spec it holds no ghost; the mock below is the placeholder look that a later spec will fill. A ghost is a person hidden by averages. One ghost = ghost.size residents (1,000 in the current params.json; the UI reads the value, it does not hard-code it). The exact total is always text: "5 ghosts = 5,000 residents".

Ghost_Layer placeholder on the map (illustrative counts)

Display cap: 12 glyphs per district (proposal). Above the cap a "+N" label is shown (Sanchong: 31 ghosts, 12 shown, "+19"). Glyph size 13 px, white body, dark outline, placed on a fixed grid around the district centroid (no random placement). Never decoration: the glyph count follows the engine ghost count.

Ghost glyph and text forms

+19

31 ghosts = 31,000 residents (District_Panel, exact)

Accessible forms: the glyph group is hidden from screen readers; the district accessible name and the District_Panel row state the exact number.

Risk escalation: 4 levels per district (thresholds are PROPOSALS)

The level of a district follows its ghost count: level 1 from 1 ghost, level 2 from 5, level 3 from 15, level 4 from 30. Every level adds a visible text label ("Risk level 3 of 4"). Effects stay inside the district shape or the District_Panel header; nothing covers the full screen, and the total animated area is far below 25 percent of the viewport. The only animation is a slow interference stripe drift in level 3 and 4 districts (one cycle per 2.4 s, about 0.4 Hz, far under 3 flashes per second) and a 400 ms ghost fade-in.
