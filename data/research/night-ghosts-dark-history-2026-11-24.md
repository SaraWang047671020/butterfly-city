# Dark historical events in Taipei City and New Taipei City: sourced candidate list

Purpose: a list of candidate memorial points for the city-simulation game. The product owner decides which ones to use.
Scope: Qing era to 2025. Taipei City (臺北市) and New Taipei City (新北市) only.
Every source was accessed on **2026-11-24**. Each row is marked "acc. 2026-11-24".
Rows marked **[in game period]** happened between 2015 and 2025.

Notes on method:
- Each row is based on at least one source that was actually checked: zh.wikipedia (with its references), a government site, a memorial institution, or major Taiwanese news (CNA 中央社, PTS 公視, LTN 自由, UDN 聯合, TVBS, The Reporter 報導者, etc.).
- When sources disagree, the row gives both figures or says the detail is uncertain. No numbers were estimated or filled in.
- The descriptions are neutral. Perpetrators of crimes are not named. Politicians are not named unless their name is part of the official event name.
- Sensitivity tags used:
  - `historical, commemorated`
  - `political, contested`
  - `political, victims' families alive`
  - `disaster`
  - `disaster, private victims`
  - `private victims, recent`
  - `displacement, contested`
  - `folklore`
