# PART 1: Memory scenes (disasters and places of isolation)

## 2.1 葛樂禮颱風水災 (Typhoon Gloria, 1963-09-10/11), Taipei basin

**Representative place chosen: 大同區, 延平北路 (大稻埕), Taipei City.**
- **Why:** a 1963 film in the national film archive names 延平北路 and 成都路 as flooded streets. It also names the low-lying 大同區 and 雙園區 as under several feet of water. That makes it the only place-level record found from the time itself.
- **Alternative:** 三重/蘆洲 (New Taipei). Taipei City's 水利處 says these areas became 「水鄉澤國」, but no source from 1963 itself was found for them.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 延平北路二段, 大同區 (大稻埕), an ordinary city street. No flood marker was found. |
| Address | 臺北市大同區延平北路二段 (a street reference, not a single address) |
| Lat/Lon | 25.05797, 121.51152 (approximate street point) |
| Coordinate source | OSM Nominatim, way 199980019 (secondary). No official flood-point coordinate was found. |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What it was.** 葛樂禮 (GLORIA) was typhoon No. 196314. It affected Taiwan on 1963-09-11, and the land warning ran from 1963-09-08 22:00 to 1963-09-11 22:00. The record notes severe flooding with deaths and missing persons in central and northern Taiwan.
   - Source: 中央氣象署 颱風資料庫, typhoon 196314, https://rdc28.cwa.gov.tw/TDB/public/typhoon_detail?typhoon_id=196314 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened here.** A contemporary film records up to 450 mm of rain in Taipei and flooded streets on 延平北路 and 成都路. It records low-lying 大同區 and 雙園區 under several feet of water, and 石門水庫 starting releases at 05:45 on 09-11.
   - Source: 國家文化記憶庫 「葛樂禮颱風過境本省北部受災」 (film, 台灣電影文化公司; catalogued by 國家電影及視聽文化中心), https://tcmb.culture.tw/zh-tw/detail?indexCode=Culture_Media&id=753880 (accessed 2026-11-24, official platform)
3. **Across the basin.** Taipei City's 水利處 says that during 葛樂禮 several parts of the 淡水河 basin flooded. It names 三重 and 蘆洲 as 「水鄉澤國」, and 浮洲里 婦聯一、二村 as flooded one storey high.
   - Source: 臺北市政府工務局水利工程處 「臺北防洪計畫」, https://heo.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=6C905A031153DF13 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
4. **Who was affected.** No official casualty count was found.
   - NOWnews (2024-07-22) gives 224 dead, 450 injured and 88 missing, with no named source.
   - Taipei Times (2021-10-24) gives about 200 deaths and more than 21,000 ha flooded in the capital region.
   - Sources: NOWnews 「凱米颱風路徑很像！台灣歷年4大「西北颱」釀300死　大台北變汪洋」, https://www.nownews.com/news/6476741 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary); Taipei Times "Taiwan in Time: Cutting out the river bends", https://taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2021/10/24/2003766662 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary)
5. **How it is remembered.** Taipei's flood-control history page records 葛樂禮 among the floods behind the 臺北地區防洪計畫. No memorial or flood marker for 1963 was found.
   - Source: same 水利處 page as step 3 (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: casualty figures (official count not found), named victims, a flood-height marker, or a memorial.
- Invention is acceptable for night ambience, such as rain and water lines on shophouse walls, as long as it is clearly labelled atmosphere.
- Sensitivity: low to medium. The event is 63 years old, but elderly survivors are alive.

## 2.2 海山煤礦災變 (1984, 土城)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | Former 海山煤礦 建安坑 area at the end of 永寧路, 土城區. Buildings are reported demolished, and part of the land was sold for development. |
| Address (official, 1984) | 原臺北縣土城鄉永寧村永寧路66號 海山煤礦 (監察院 report) |
| Lat/Lon | Exact point not found. Approximate street reference: 24.95632, 121.42916 (a segment of 永寧路, 永寧里). This is **not** the mine portal. |
| Coordinate source | OSM Nominatim, way 198285746 (secondary). The address 永寧路66號 itself did not geocode. |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What the place was.** 海山煤礦公司 worked the 建安坑, 媽祖坑 and 建福坑 pits. Its mining area spanned 土城鄉 and part of 三峽鎮 (947.0218 ha).
   - Source: 監察院 調查報告 (葉大華、賴鼎銘; 海山、煤山及海一煤礦三大礦災真相、善款管理、罹難及倖存礦工家庭照護等情案), https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/72322 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened and when.** A coal-dust explosion occurred inside 建安坑 on 民國73年6月20日 (1984-06-20).
   - The same report's summary paragraphs and the press release give 6月10日. The detailed section, Wikipedia and a 1984 NZ press report ("on Wednesday"; 1984-06-20 was a Wednesday) support 06-20.
   - Use **1984-06-20** and keep the note on the discrepancy.
   - Sources: 監察院 report above (official); 監察院 新聞稿 2025-09-23, https://www.cy.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=125&s=34859 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
3. **Who was affected.** 74 workers died. The original investigation recorded 3 injured, while a later 臺北縣 report cited 43 hospitalised.
   - Source: 監察院 report above (official)
4. **The investigation.** The official investigation attributed the disaster to human error (人為疏失). Its report was not actively made public for about 40 years. 地礦中心 published the full report with its attachments on its website on 2025-06-10, after the 監察院 inquiry.
   - Sources: 監察院 新聞稿 and report above (official)
5. **How it is remembered.** PTS reports that miners' memorial tablets (牌位) for the victims survive at the old site. The 監察院 (2025) asked agencies to preserve mining-history records, fund oral history of affected families, and hold commemorations. No official memorial monument was found.
   - Sources: PTS 「記憶‧煤鄉｜台灣煤業歷史如何保存？【我們的島】」 (2025-02-26), https://news.pts.org.tw/article/739099 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary); 監察院 report (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: names, last words, a cause beyond the official finding, the location of the tablets, or blame on any person.
- Sensitivity: **high**. Survivors, widows and children are alive, and the donation and accountability questions are still active (監察院 2025).

## 2.3 煤山煤礦災變 (1984, 瑞芳)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | Former 煤山煤礦 area in 瑞芳區 (九份 / 水湳洞 area). Its present state was not found. |
| Address (official) | Mining area at 「臺北縣瑞芳鎮庚子寮九份水南洞地方」, 170 ha (監察院 report). No street address. |
| Lat/Lon | Not found for the mine site. Approximate area reference: 25.12538, 121.86230 (hamlet 水湳洞). This is **not** the portal. |
| Coordinate source | OSM Nominatim, node 3586391795 (secondary) |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What the place was.** 煤山煤礦 was formed when 碧山煤礦 merged and was renamed, with its mining area at 瑞芳鎮 庚子寮 九份 水南洞.
   - Source: 監察院 調查報告, https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/72322 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened and when.** On 1984-07-10 (around 12:40 by the official account) an underground fire broke out in the 又斜坑 section. 125 people were trapped, and recovery work lasted 8 days and nights, ending on 07-18.
   - Source: 監察院 report above (official)
3. **Who was affected.** 103 died and 22 were injured, according to the investigation summarised by the 監察院.
   - Source: 監察院 report and 新聞稿 https://www.cy.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=125&s=34859 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
4. **The investigation.** The investigation attributed the fire to an electrical fault and recorded the disaster as human error (人為疏失). Overseas experts (from South Africa) assisted.
   - Source: 監察院 report above (official)
5. **How it is remembered.** The 2025 監察院 findings ask for the history to be preserved and commemorated. No memorial monument was found.
   - Source: 監察院 新聞稿 (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: survivors' experiences (some survivors had lasting injuries), names, or voices.
- Sensitivity: **high**. Survivors and families are alive.

## 2.4 海一（海山一坑）煤礦災變 (1984-12-05). The district is **三峽**, not 土城.

**District resolved:** the 監察院 report states 「三峽鎮海一煤礦」, with its mining area at 「臺北縣三峽鎮成福里溪東南里地方」, 826.35 ha. The relief committee was set up by 原三峽鎮公所.
- The confusion with 土城 probably comes from the mine's name (海山一坑) and the neighbouring 海山煤礦, whose area also reached 三峽.
- The report's phrase 「成福里溪東南里」 may mean 成福里, 溪東里 and 溪南里. It is quoted as written.

   - Source: 監察院 調查報告, https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/72322 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)

   - Source: 監察院 report and 新聞稿, https://www.cy.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=125&s=34859 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)

   - Source: 監察院 report, footnote 2 (official)
4. **The response.** 原三峽鎮公所 ran the 「送愛心到海一」 donation drive and set up a fund-management committee.
   - Source: 監察院 report (official)
5. **How it is remembered.** Commemoration is part of the 2025 監察院 recommendations. No memorial site was found.
   - Source: 監察院 新聞稿 (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: names, the cause (it is officially undetermined), or a memorial.
- Sensitivity: **high**. Families are alive.

## 2.11 象神颱風 汐止水災 (2000-10-31 / 11-01)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 汐止區 (New Taipei), along the 基隆河. No single official flood point was found. |
| Address | 新北市汐止區 (district reference) |
| Lat/Lon | 25.06416, 121.65875 (district reference point, not a flood point) |
| Coordinate source | OSM Nominatim, relation 1297097 (secondary) |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What it was.** 象神 (XANGSANE) was typhoon No. 200020. It affected Taiwan on 2000-11-01. Heavy rain from its outer circulation combined with a front caused severe flooding in parts of 汐止, Taipei City, Keelung and Yilan. The CWA record gives 64 dead in total.
   - Source: 中央氣象署 颱風資料庫 200020, https://rdc28.cwa.gov.tw/TDB/public/typhoon_detail?typhoon_id=200020 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened along the 基隆河.** About 465 ha flooded along the 基隆河, including Taipei's 文山, 內湖 and 南港, 臺北縣 瑞芳 and 汐止, and Keelung's 暖暖 and 七堵. The 基隆河 entry records 59 dead; this differs from the CWA nationwide total.
   - Source: 經濟部水利署第十河川分署 「治理沿革- 員山子分洪」, https://www.wra.gov.tw/wra10/cp.aspx?n=32140 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
3. **Scale in 汐止.** About two-thirds of 汐止 urban area was reported flooded.
   - Source: udn (聯合報系), https://udn.com/news/story/120910/8828015 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary). The figure was not confirmed in an official document.
4. **How it is remembered.** 象神 led directly to the 員山子分洪 diversion. The Executive Yuan approved it on 2001-05-11, construction began on 2002-06-24, it first diverted water on 2004-09-11, and it was completed on 2005-10-28.
   - Source: 水利署第十河川分署 page above (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: specific drowned persons or buildings, or casualty figures for 汐止 alone (not found).
- Sensitivity: medium to high. Survivors and families are alive.

## 2.12 納莉颱風 捷運淹水 (2001-09-16/17), 臺北車站

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 臺北車站, MRT 淡水信義線 and 板南線 levels underground. The station is in operation. |
| Address | 臺北市中正區 臺北車站 (忠孝西路一段/北平西路) |
| Lat/Lon | 25.04634, 121.51788 (MRT 臺北車站 exit M3) |
| Coordinate source | Taipei Metro open data (official) |
| Flood marker 「記得九一七嗎？」 | **Not found.** No official or news source confirming this marker at 臺北車站 was found. PTS (2025) says only that some Taipei MRT stations keep a 納莉 historic water-level line, without naming 臺北車站. Verify on site before use. |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What it was.** 納莉 (NARI) was typhoon No. 200116. It made landfall around 21:40 on 2001-09-16 between 三貂角 and 頭城 and stayed over Taiwan for about 49 hours. The CWA record says the Taipei MRT and 臺北車站 flooded.
   - Source: 中央氣象署 颱風資料庫 200116, https://rdc28.cwa.gov.tw/TDB/public/typhoon_detail?typhoon_id=200116 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened here.** 16 underground MRT stations, the 南港 depot and the operations control centre flooded. Water reached 臺北車站 by two routes: via 昆陽 on the 板南線, and via the shared railway tunnel from 松山 on the 淡水線. The full network resumed on 2001-12-15.
   - Source: TVBS 「北捷30週年／納莉颱風慘況觸目驚心！12站一夜沒了　前段長憶最難忘時刻」 (2026-03-28), https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/3161340 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary)
3. **Who was affected.** Nationwide, 94 people died and 10 were missing (CWA).
   - Source: CWA 200116 (official); PTS 「歷史上的今天》納莉颱風：那一年，台北變成了湖」 (2025-09-16), https://news.pts.org.tw/article/770471 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary)
4. **How it is remembered.** After 納莉, Taipei MRT raised the freeboard in its flood standard from 50 cm to 110 cm. It also checks designs against 納莉 flood levels, and it added watertight doors and full-section flood bulkheads.
   - Source: 「臺北捷運系統防洪設計及措施」 (文圖/許慶珩), Taipei City Government upload, https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/388/relfile/19519/7926622/8d450e91-e2b9-440a-91b4-ec91a1dd2562.pdf (accessed 2026-11-24, official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: the 「記得九一七嗎？」 marker text or its height (unverified), or deaths inside the MRT (none found).
- Sensitivity: medium.

## 4.1 臺北監獄（臺北刑務所）圍牆遺蹟

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | Remaining north and south walls beside the Chunghwa Telecom property, 金山南路二段 / 愛國東路, 大安區 |
| Address (official) | 原臺北監獄東、西、南、北側之圍牆，鄰近金山南路二段、金山南路二段102巷、愛國東路210巷、愛國東路21巷、金山南路二段44巷 |
| Lat/Lon | 25.03207, 121.52618 |
| Coordinate source | BOCH open data, case 19980325000006 (official) |
| Related site | 舊台北監獄尖底明溝 (歷史建築, 2023-12-04), 金山南路二段94號, 25.03104, 121.52650, BOCH case 20231215000001 (official) |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What the place was.** The prison was built around the 1910s, under Japanese rule, on a radial plan. Its walls were largely built of stone from the demolished 臺北府城 wall (安山岩, 唭哩岸石).
   - Source: 國家文化資產網 「臺北監獄圍牆遺蹟」, http://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/advanceSearch/monument/19980325000006 (data via BOCH open data; accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **Why it matters.** Taipei City designated the wall a monument on 1998-03-25 (府民三字第8701538601號), with the boundary revised on 2014-06-04. The designation cites its value as evidence of modern judicial history and its link to people imprisoned for resisting Japanese rule.
   - Source: same record (official)
3. **When it ended.** The prison was originally on 愛國東路 and moved to its present site (龜山) in 民國52年 (1963).
   - Source: 法務部矯正署臺北監獄 新聞稿 (2017-10-02), https://www.tpp.moj.gov.tw/296713/296792/296794/360945 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
4. **Who was held here.** The Korean independence activist 趙明河 was executed at this prison on 1928-10-10. This is a historical figure, not a private victim.
   - Source: CNA 「韓國抗日英雄趙明河在台逝世92週年 學者憑弔」 (2020-10-10), https://www.cna.com.tw/news/acul/202010100150.aspx (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary)
5. **How it is remembered today.** The wall remains are a 直轄市定古蹟. An excavated drainage ditch was also listed as a historic building in 2023.
   - Source: BOCH cases 19980325000006 and 20231215000001 (official)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: named prisoners, executions or White Terror cases tied to this site beyond the sources.
- Sensitivity: medium. Descendants of political prisoners may be alive.

## 4.2 樂生療養院 (新莊)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 衛生福利部樂生療養院 (still a hospital and residence), with the preserved old campus |
| Address (official) | 新北市新莊區中正路794號 |
| Lat/Lon | 25.02221, 121.40923 (文化景觀). The 歷史建築 record gives 25.02216, 121.40910. |
| Coordinate source | BOCH open data, cases 20090907000002 (文化景觀) and 20090907000001 (歷史建築) (official) |

**Investigation steps**
1. **What the place was.** It was founded in 1930 (昭和五年) under compulsory Japanese government policy, as an isolation institution for Hansen's disease (漢生病) patients. It was a lifelong home, with administration, medical, residential, religious, market, dining and bath buildings. Early on it was enclosed by barbed wire.
   - Source: 國家文化資產網 「新莊樂生療養院」 (歷史建築), http://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/advanceSearch/historicalBuilding/20090907000001 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **What happened and when.** In 1954 it was over capacity and was expanded with US aid. By 1985 it had 56 patient dormitories and 970 beds. After protests between 1994 and 2007, 49 buildings were kept.
   - Source: same record (official)
3. **Who was affected.** The designation text says more than a thousand patients lived and died here. At the time of listing (2009), one to two hundred patients still lived there.
   - Source: 國家文化資產網 「新莊樂生療養院」 (文化景觀), http://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/advanceSearch/culturalLandscape/20090907000002 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
4. **Recognition.** The 漢生病病患人權保障及補償條例 took effect on 2008-08-13. On 2009-02-12 the Premier announced a public apology to all Hansen's disease patients and their families.
   - Source: 衛生福利部 「樂生園區巡禮」, https://mohw.gov.tw/cp-16-27851-1.html (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
5. **How it is remembered.** It was registered as both 文化景觀 and 歷史建築 by 臺北縣 (now New Taipei) on 2009-09-07.
   - Sources: BOCH records above (official); 監察院 documents note the 2009 listing and its status as a 世界遺產潛力點, for example https://cybsbox.cy.gov.tw/CYBSBoxSSL/edoc/download/54397 (accessed 2026-11-24, official; content summarised by search, not read in full)

**Room for invention**
- Do not invent: patients' words, names or medical details. Do not use horror framing (the disease and the stigma are real history).
- Sensitivity: **very high**. Residents still live on site, and families are alive.

| Lat/Lon | 25.04367, 121.50891 (a point on 中華路一段; the market was about 1.2 km long) |
| Coordinate source | OSM Nominatim, way 52513625 (secondary). No official point was found. Wikipedia gives 25°02′41″N 121°30′33″E (secondary). |

**Facts**
1. **Origin.** After 1949, temporary bamboo and wood shelters built along the railway on 中華路 grew into a large unauthorised settlement. These were replaced by the market.
   - Source: 國家文化記憶庫 「中華路改建商場前的街景-遠觀違建商店鋪」 (original held by 臺北市立文獻館), https://tcmb.culture.tw/zh-tw/detail?indexCode=MOCCOLLECTIONS&id=144000010449 (accessed 2026-11-24, official platform)
2. **Built.** Building began in 1960 and the completion ceremony was held on 1961-04-22. There were eight connected three-storey buildings, with 1,644 tenants of roughly 2 坪 each.
   - Sources: 國家文化記憶庫 「中華商場」, https://tcmb.culture.tw/zh-tw/detail?id=300679&indexCode=Culture_Place (accessed 2026-11-24, official platform, contributor text); 「北市中華商場昨擧行落成禮」 (1961-04-23), https://tcmb.culture.tw/zh-tw/detail?indexCode=Culture_Object&id=301814 (accessed 2026-11-24, official platform)
3. **Daily life.** Stalls sold antiques, stamps, imported goods and consumer electronics, and there were fortune tellers.
   - Source: 國家文化記憶庫 「中華商場」 (above)
4. **End.** It was demolished in 1992 for MRT construction and urban planning, and the site became a tree-lined boulevard. Wikipedia gives the demolition as 1992-10-20 to 10-30, building by building.
   - Sources: tcmb 144000010449 (official platform); Wikipedia 中華商場, https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/中華商場 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary)

## 4.5 寶藏巖聚落 (汀州路三段230巷, 中正區)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 寶藏巖國際藝術村 plus 寶藏家園 residents; the temple 寶藏巖 (古蹟) lies within the settlement |
| Address | 臺北市中正區汀州路三段230巷. The BOCH settlement record lists land lots 福和段二小段690、691、694、695-1、695-2地號. The temple is at 汀州路3段230巷23號. |
| Lat/Lon | Settlement 25.01092, 121.53225; temple 25.01056, 121.53323 |

**Facts**
1. **Built.** The settlement formed in the 1960s–70s as informal self-built housing, mainly by retired veterans, on the slopes of 小觀音山 by the 新店溪. The entrance barracks are the only surviving military building nearby.
   - Sources: 國家文化資產網 「寶藏巖聚落」, http://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/advanceSearch/groupsOfBuildings/20110527000001 (accessed 2026-11-24, official); 寶藏巖國際藝術村 「關於藝術村」, https://www.artistvillage.org/about.php (accessed 2026-11-24, official: run by 臺北市文化基金會)
2. **Threat.** In July 1980 the city moved the area from water-source protection land into a planned park, which put the houses at risk of demolition. Preservation campaigns followed.
   - Source: 臺北市政府文化局 「寶藏巖國際藝術村」, https://culture.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=20994098C3459E18 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
3. **Change.**
   - 2004: registered as a 歷史建築 (Taipei's first settlement listed this way).
   - 2011-05-27: registered as a settlement (北市文化二字第10030320800號).
   - 2018-08-31: registered as a 聚落建築群.
   - Sources: BOCH record (official); 文化局 page (official)
4. **Today.** Repair work began in late 2006, and the artist village opened on 2010-10-02. The site combines art residencies with 寶藏家園 housing for original residents (「藝居共生」).
   - Sources: artistvillage.org and 文化局 page (official)

## 4.6 文萌樓 (歸綏街139號, 大同區). Handle with care.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Site today | 市定古蹟 「歸綏街文萌樓」, privately owned. Its current use after 2017 was not confirmed. |
| Address (official) | 臺北市大同區歸綏街139號 |
| Lat/Lon | 25.05842, 121.51478 |
| Coordinate source | BOCH open data, case 20061220000001 (official) |

**Facts (stated neutrally)**
1. **Built.** It is a 1930s–40s colonial-era shophouse with Baroque-derived elements and yellow-green tiles. Its interior partitions still reflect its use as a licensed brothel (公娼館).
   - Source: 國家文化資產網 「歸綏街文萌樓」, http://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/advanceSearch/monument/20061220000001 (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
2. **Daily life and status.** The official record states that it housed a licensed brothel from 1941. The record describes it as part of the memory of the river-port city's sex industry, and as a centre of the movement against abolishing licensed prostitution.
   - Source: same record (official)
3. **Change.**
   - 1997: Taipei City abolished licensed prostitution, and the building became the base of the 公娼自救會 during the 1997–98 protests.
   - 2001: the city's last licensed brothels closed after a buffer period (the last night was 2001-03-28, per the National Center of Photography record).
   - Sources: 臺北市政府文化局 publication page 「市定古蹟歸綏街文萌樓調查研究及保存計畫」 (2008), https://culture.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=2699A54FC44BC901&sms=C79CCD04698D9177&s=228E6BCE39AEDC24 (accessed 2026-11-24, official); 國家攝影文化中心 〈末代公娼〉 NCP2016-026-0035, https://collections.culture.tw/ncop_collectionsweb/collection.aspx?GID=MZMQMDMRMCMAMRMLME (accessed 2026-11-24, official)
4. **Today.** It was designated a 市定古蹟 on 2006-12-20 (府文化二字第09530346200號). The building changed hands in 2011, and after a lawsuit the 日日春 association had to leave (Supreme Court, 2017).
   - Sources: BOCH record (official); PTS 我們的島 「老屋的困局-都更來襲」 (2014-01-27), https://ourisland.pts.org.tw/content/1460 (accessed 2026-11-24, secondary); 2017 ruling per search summaries of PNN/PTS project pages, https://pnn.pts.org.tw/project/inpage/1352/83/224 (secondary; not read in full)
   - Current use (2018–2026): **not found**.
