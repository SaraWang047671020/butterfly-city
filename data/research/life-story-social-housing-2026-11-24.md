# Social housing allocation research: Taipei City & New Taipei City, 2015–2025

All pages accessed **2026-11-24** unless noted. Status labels: **official** = government document or page; **secondary** = news or NGO quoting officials; **not found** = searched, nothing usable.
ROC year + 1911 = CE year (e.g. 113年 = 2024). 萬 = NT$10,000.
"Fetched" means I read the page or PDF myself. "Snippet" means the figure came only from a search-engine summary because the page blocked automated access. Treat snippet figures as weaker.

**Important caveats for the game formula (units ÷ applicants)**
- Taipei counts are often *entries* (籤數/件數), and from 2021 applicants could pick several sites in one application (up to 14 in 2022). Applicants are therefore double-counted across sites, so per-site odds understate a person's overall chance.
- Many 2024–2025 news figures for Taipei and New Taipei are **central-government (國家住都中心) projects located in the city**, not city-run projects. These are flagged below.
- No source gave applicant counts split into general (一般戶) and priority (弱勢/優先) households. Only quotas of units are available.

## 3. Eligibility rules and income limits

**Taipei core rules** (出租辦法 art. 4 in the 2017 version, art. 5 in the 2023 version; official, fetched):
- Age: 2017 version "aged 20 or over" (年滿二十歲). 2023 version "adult" (已成年; Taiwan's age of majority became 18 on 2023-01-01).
- Registered in Taipei, OR not registered but studying or working in Taipei.
- No household member owns a home in Taipei, New Taipei, Keelung or Taoyuan. Not already renting public, national or social housing in those cities.
- Household income below the city's **50th-percentile** household average for the application year, AND per-person monthly income ≤ **3.5×** the Taipei minimum living cost.
- Real estate below the city's mid-low-income property limit.

**New Taipei core rules** (承租辦法 art. 5, 2021-10-13; official): household registration in New Taipei OR studying or working there; income below the NTPC 50th percentile and ≤3.5× minimum living cost per person; no home in New Taipei, Taipei, Taoyuan or Keelung.
