## 2. Eligibility rules

### 2a. Rules by period (official)

Main source E1: 內政部, 「整合住宅補貼資源實施方案」 (110年4月 revision), §貳.五, https://www.nlma.gov.tw/uploads/files/0afab4b2444d39f041633e9c638dcd65.pdf

| Period | Income rule for rent subsidy | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2007–2014 (96–103) | Household annual income below the 50th-percentile household average **and** per-person monthly income below 3.5 × 最低生活費. Asset limits added from 2013 | E1 |
| 2015–2018 (104–107) | Central-funded quota: household annual income below the 20th-percentile household average **or** per-person monthly income below 1.5 × 最低生活費. Cities could set looser local rules (up to the 50th percentile, or 2.5× or 3.5×) at their own cost | E1 |
| 2019 (108) | The 20th-percentile household-income route was removed. Per-person monthly income must be below 1.5 × 最低生活費. Reason given: in 2018 a single person in Taipei earning over NT$70,000 a month could qualify under Taipei's 20th-percentile limit of NT$880,000 | E1 |
| 2020–2021 (109–110) | Per-person monthly income below **2.5 ×** 最低生活費. Movable-asset limit 2× the 中低收入戶 limit. Merged with the youth / marriage pilot | E1 |
| From 2022-07-01 (300億 program) | Per-person monthly income below **3 ×** 最低生活費 (raised from 2.5×) | 行政院 pages (2022-06-30 and 2024-08-23) above |

Other conditions, 300億 program (official, from the 115年度 application form, below):
- Household members (applicant, spouse, minor children, wards) own no home.
- The applicant actually lives in the rented unit.
- The lease has started, or starts within 60 days.
- The landlord is not a family member or direct relative.
- No double housing assistance.

Taipei 2018 conditions (official, Taipei news release 2018-02-14):
- Republic of China (ROC) national, aged 20 or over.
- Married, **or** registered in the same household as a direct relative, **or** single and **aged 40 or over** (with an orphan-sibling exception).
- No home owned by any household member.

So before 2019, single people under 40 generally could not apply. The 2019 youth pilot and the 2020 merger opened the scheme to them. The 2019 pilot (協助單身青年及鼓勵婚育租金補貼試辦方案, approved 2019-06-21) covered single people aged 20–40, couples married within 2 years and families with minor children: 19,670 applied, 13,494 approved, closed 2019-09-27 (E1, §壹.七).

Taipei top-up from 2022 (加碼), 2025 table (official, Taipei plan URL in §2b):
- Requires per-person income ≤ 1.5 × 最低生活費: NT$20,379 (step 1) or NT$30,569 (step 2).
- For 2–3 person households the city tops up the central amount to NT$7,000 (step 1) or NT$6,300 (step 2).
- For 4+ person households it tops up to NT$11,000 or NT$10,300.
- The subsidy can never exceed the actual rent.

Duration: before 2022 the subsidy lasted 1 year and households re-applied each year (E1 §貳.六.7). Under the 300億 program it runs to the end of the calendar year, households re-apply each year, and NLMA may carry data forward (2026 form).
